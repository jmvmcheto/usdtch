USDT CH (USDTCH) is a fixed-supply community and utility token on BNB Smart Chain created for the CHETO ecosystem, with potential use in community rewards, promotions, merchandise, events and digital experiences.

Important: USDTCH is independently created. It is not affiliated with, issued by, backed by, or redeemable through Tether. It is not a USD-pegged stablecoin and does not represent a claim on U.S. dollars.

Purpose

USDTCH was created as an experimental community token for the CHETO ecosystem. Its intended role is to explore blockchain-based rewards, access, promotions and future digital experiences associated with the brand.

Token design

  • Fixed total supply of 100,000 tokens.
  • No additional mint function.
  • No transfer taxes.
  • No blacklist functionality.
  • No administrative mechanism to alter balances.
